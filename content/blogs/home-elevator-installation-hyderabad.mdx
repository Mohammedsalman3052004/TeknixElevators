### Types of Home Elevators Suitable for Hyderabad Homes

                

Every home is different. The right elevator for a compact 2-floor row house in LB Nagar is not the same as what works best for a 4-floor villa in Jubilee Hills. Here's a breakdown of the main types:

                

**1. Traction Elevators (Machine-Room-Less / MRL)**

                

The most popular choice for modern Hyderabad homes. Traction elevators use steel ropes or belts powered by an electric motor. The MRL (machine-room-less) variant eliminates the need for a dedicated machine room, saving valuable space.

                

- **Best for:** Homes with 3–6 floors, modern apartments, villas
- **Key benefits:** Smooth and quiet ride, energy-efficient, fast
- **Teknix models:** Optima, Vertix

                

**2. Hydraulic Elevators**

                

Hydraulic lifts use pressurised fluid to raise and lower the cabin. They are reliable, smooth, and do not require heavy overhead structures — making them very suitable for Hyderabad's many independent villa constructions.

                

- **Best for:** 2–4 floor homes, bungalows, independent houses
- **Key benefits:** No overhead machine room needed, gentle ride, robust
- **Teknix model:** Hydratek

                

**3. Energy-Efficient Elevators**

                

Designed for eco-conscious homeowners and green-certified buildings, these elevators use regenerative drive technology that recovers electricity during the cabin's descent and feeds it back into the building's power supply.

                

- **Best for:** IGBC/LEED certified homes, solar-powered houses, sustainability-focused buyers
- **Key benefits:** Up to 35% energy savings, reduced electricity bills, quiet operation
- **Teknix model:** Greentek X2

                

**4. Luxury Villa Elevators**

                

A home elevator does not have to blend into the background. Luxury villa lifts are designed as interior features — with custom glass panels, premium wood or marble finishes, ambient lighting, and mirror walls that complement your home's design language.

                

- **Best for:** Premium villas in Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Kokapet, Film Nagar
- **Key benefits:** Bespoke aesthetics, premium materials, high resale appeal
- **Teknix model:** Villa Matek

### The Home Elevator Installation Process — Step by Step

                

Understanding what happens between "I want a lift" and "the lift is ready" helps you plan properly and avoid surprises.

                

**Step 1: Site Survey**

                

A qualified engineer visits your home to assess the available space, floor-to-floor height, structural load capacity, and shaft feasibility. This is typically free of charge with reputable suppliers.

                

**Step 2: Product Selection and Design**

                

Based on the site survey, you choose the elevator type, capacity, cabin dimensions, and interior finish. For luxury models, this is where customisation happens — cabin materials, lighting, door style, and floor design are all selected.

                

**Step 3: Civil Work (Shaft Construction)**

                

If your home does not already have a shaft, one needs to be constructed. This involves a pit at the base (typically 300–500 mm deep) and a shaft rising to the top floor with the correct headroom clearance. Civil work usually takes 2–4 weeks and is coordinated between your civil contractor and the elevator team.

                

**Step 4: Elevator Installation**

                

Once the shaft is ready, Teknix's installation team moves in. Mechanical components, guide rails, cabin, door systems, and electrical wiring are all installed. This stage typically takes 7–14 days depending on the model and number of floors.

                

**Step 5: Testing, Inspection and Commissioning**

                

Every installation undergoes rigorous load testing, safety system checks, and operational testing before handover. Teknix conducts this internally before the required government inspection.

                

**Step 6: Handover and Training**

                

You receive a complete orientation on operating the elevator, understanding safety features, and knowing what to do in case of a power cut or emergency. Your AMC agreement is also finalised at this stage.

                

### 6 Key Things to Look for Before Buying a Home Elevator in Hyderabad

                

**1. Safety Certifications**

                

Always verify that your elevator meets: IS 14665 (Indian standard for electric traction lifts), BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) certification, CE Marking (European safety compliance), and NBC (National Building Code) compliance. Teknix elevators are co-engineered with SRH Aufzüge GmbH, a German manufacturer and VFA-Interlift member, ensuring compliance with both European and Indian safety standards.

                

**2. Shaft Space and Civil Requirements**

                

Different elevator types have different minimum space requirements. A standard 2-person home lift needs a shaft footprint of approximately 1200 mm × 1200 mm, while a wheelchair-accessible cabin requires more. Confirm these dimensions with your civil contractor early to avoid expensive rework.

                

**3. Load Capacity**

                

Select your elevator's capacity based on your household's actual needs:

                

- 200–320 kg — 2 to 4 passengers, compact cabins
- 320–480 kg — 4 to 6 passengers, ideal for families and wheelchair access
- For wheelchair or mobility aid use, opt for a minimum cabin size of 1100 mm × 1400 mm

                

**4. Power Backup and Safety Systems**

                

Hyderabad, like many Indian cities, experiences occasional power outages. Ensure your elevator includes:

                

- **ARD (Automatic Rescue Device)** — moves the cabin to the nearest floor and opens doors during a power failure
- **UPS or battery backup** — keeps the lift operational during short outages
- **Emergency alarm and intercom** — for passenger communication during a stoppage

                

**5. Warranty and After-Sales Support**

                

This is one of the most overlooked factors. Ask specifically: What is the warranty period on parts and labour? Is an AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) available? What is the response time for emergency breakdown calls in Hyderabad? Are spare parts stocked locally? Teknix has dedicated service support in Hyderabad with local spare parts inventory and trained technicians — ensuring fast response times and minimal disruption to your household.

                

**6. Reputation and Track Record**

                

Before committing, check: How long has the company been operating? Do they have completed installations in Hyderabad you can visit or verify? Are testimonials and references available? Do they have a physical office or service centre in the city?

### Frequently Asked Questions — Home Elevators in Hyderabad

                

                  

Can I install a home elevator in an existing Hyderabad home without major renovation?

                  

Yes. Retrofit installations are possible with careful planning. External shaft construction or compact designs like the Hydratek, which requires minimal pit depth and no overhead machine room, are excellent options for existing homes. A site survey will confirm exact feasibility.

                

                

                  

How long does it take to install a home elevator in Hyderabad?

                  

Once the shaft is ready, installation typically takes 7–14 days. If a new shaft needs to be built, add 2–4 weeks for civil work. The total timeline from site survey to handover is usually 6–8 weeks.

                

                

                  

Does Hyderabad have specific regulations for home elevator installation?

                  

For private residential properties in Hyderabad, approvals depend on GHMC (Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation) building plan sanctions and HMDA norms. Teknix's team will guide you through all necessary compliance steps at no additional charge.

                

                

                  

What happens to the elevator during a power cut?

                  

All Teknix elevators are equipped with an ARD (Automatic Rescue Device) that automatically brings the cabin to the nearest floor and opens the doors safely in the event of a power failure. Battery backup systems are also available as an add-on.

                

                

                  

Is there a minimum floor height requirement for installing a home lift?

                  

Standard floor-to-floor height for most Hyderabad homes (typically 3 to 3.5 metres) is sufficient. Your site survey will confirm compatibility and any adjustments needed.

                

                

                  

What is covered under an AMC for a home elevator?

                  

A Teknix AMC typically includes scheduled preventive maintenance visits, safety inspections, lubrication and adjustment of mechanical components, and priority response for breakdown calls. Specific coverage terms are discussed and confirmed at contract signing.

                

                

                  

How energy-efficient are modern home elevators?

                  

Modern traction elevators with VFD (Variable Frequency Drive) motors consume significantly less electricity than older models. Teknix's Greentek X2 uses regenerative drive technology that can reduce energy consumption by up to 35% — ideal for Hyderabad homes looking to lower electricity costs.

                

                

                  

What is the lifespan of a home elevator?

                  

With regular maintenance and timely component servicing, a quality home elevator typically lasts 20–25 years. Major component upgrades may be needed at the 10–15 year mark depending on usage frequency and model.
