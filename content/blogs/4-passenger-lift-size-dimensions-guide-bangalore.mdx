Whether you are constructing a luxury villa in Indiranagar, retrofitting a duplex in Jayanagar, or building a boutique commercial space in HSR Layout, installing a **4-passenger lift** offers the ideal balance of vertical mobility and compact footprint. In Bangalore's evolving residential landscape, the 4-passenger elevator (carrying **272 kg to 300 kg**) remains the most sought-after capacity for urban homes.

Planning the exact shaft and cabin requirements early prevents costly structural rework and ensures compliance with local Bengaluru BBMP building bylaws and Indian Standards (IS 14665).

### Standard 4-Passenger Lift Dimensions (IS Standards)

A standard 4-passenger lift is engineered for a payload of **272 kg** (calculated per Indian Standards at 68 kg per person) up to **300 kg**.

### Dimension Matrix

| Parameter | Standard Measurement (mm) | Feet & Inches Equivalent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cabin Internal Width | 1,000 mm – 1,100 mm | ~3 ft 3 in – 3 ft 7 in |
| Cabin Internal Depth | 800 mm – 900 mm | ~2 ft 7 in – 2 ft 11 in |
| Cabin Height | 2,200 mm | ~7 ft 2 in |
| Shaft (Hoistway) Width | 1,400 mm – 1,500 mm | ~4 ft 7 in – 4 ft 11 in |
| Shaft (Hoistway) Depth | 1,300 mm – 1,400 mm | ~4 ft 3 in – 4 ft 7 in |
| Clear Door Opening | 700 mm – 800 mm | ~2 ft 3 in – 2 ft 7 in |
| Pit Depth | 1,100 mm – 1,350 mm | ~3 ft 7 in – 4 ft 5 in |
| Overhead Height | 3,600 mm – 4,200 mm | ~11 ft 9 in – 13 ft 9 in |

*Note: For specialized Machine Room-Less (MRL) gearless traction lifts, civil shaft requirements can be further optimized based on your structural layout.*

### Key Space Considerations for Bangalore Homes

**1. Civil Shaft vs. Usable Cabin Space**

The **cabin dimension** is the internal standing area for passengers. The **shaft dimension** includes the civil brick/concrete enclosure needed to accommodate guide rails, counterweights, door headers, and safety gear. As a rule of thumb, add **350 mm to 450 mm** to cabin dimensions to calculate the required civil shaft size.

**2. Door Operations for Compact Layouts**

- **Automatic Telescopic Doors:** Best suited for narrow shafts as the panels slide to one side, maximizing the entrance clear opening.
- **Automatic Center-Opening Doors:** Requires wider shaft width so panels can slide outward into both sides.
- **Manual Swing Doors:** Widely used in small home retrofits in Bangalore to conserve shaft depth, opening outward into the floor landing area.

**3. Pit & Overhead Constraints**

- **Pit Depth:** The recessed pit beneath the ground landing holds buffers and governor tensioners. While standard traction lifts need 1,100 mm to 1,350 mm, low-pit configurations are available for homes where deep excavation is restricted.
- **Overhead Clearance:** The top-floor ceiling height needed for headroom and safety clearances during maintenance.

### Advantages of Installing a 4-Passenger Lift in Bangalore

- **Fits Stairwell Cutouts:** Requires less than a 5 ft x 5 ft civil footprint, making it easy to integrate into stairwells or courtyards.
- **Single-Phase Power Compatible:** Advanced MRL gearless drives can operate on standard domestic single-phase power supplies, drastically cutting energy consumption.
- **Enhances Property Value:** Adding a home elevator in independent houses or villas across Bangalore substantially raises resale appeal and accessibility for elderly family members.
- **No Separate Machine Room Required:** Modern MRL technology places the drive inside the hoistway, eliminating the need for a rooftop machine room.
