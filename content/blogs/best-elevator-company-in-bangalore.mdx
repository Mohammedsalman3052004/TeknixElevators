### Key Features to Demand in Your Bangalore Elevator Installation

1. **Space & Pit Optimization:** Look for compact shaft requirements (under 5 ft x 5 ft for 4-passenger models) and low pit depth capabilities for retrofitting existing homes.
2. **Comprehensive Safety Package:** Ensure your elevator comes standard with an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD for power outages), infrared full-height safety door curtains, emergency intercom, and overload indicators.
3. **Energy Efficiency:** Opt for gearless permanent magnet synchronous motor (PMSM) drives that consume up to 50% less power than legacy geared lifts.
4. **Local AMC Reliability:** Choose a manufacturer with stocked spare parts within Bengaluru to avoid long repair lead times.
