### What to Check Before Installing a Lift in Bangalore

1. **Machine Room-Less (MRL) Options:** Ensure the company offers MRL technology so you do not have to waste roof space on a dedicated machine room.
2. **Power Supply Requirements:** For independent houses and villas, pick a lift capable of running smoothly on standard single-phase household power.
3. **Crucial Safety Gear:** Demand standard inclusions such as Automatic Rescue Device (ARD for power outages), infrared full-height door sensors, emergency alarm/intercom, and overload warning.
4. **Local AMC & Emergency Response:** Verify that the company has local technicians and spare parts ready in Bengaluru for rapid service response.
