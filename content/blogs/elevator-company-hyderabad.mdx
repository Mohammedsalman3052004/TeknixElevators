### Essential Factors Before Installing an Elevator in Hyderabad

1. **Space & Pit Depth Optimization:** Verify if the elevator supports compact civil shaft spaces (e.g., under 5 ft x 5 ft for 4-passenger models) and shallow pit requirements (300 mm to 1,100 mm) for retrofit villa projects.
2. **Power Requirements:** For private residences and duplexes in areas like Kondapur or Manikonda, select single-phase gearless drives that consume minimal power and reduce grid strain.
3. **Crucial Safety Features:** Ensure standard inclusion of Automatic Rescue Device (ARD for power outages), infrared full-height door sensors, emergency intercom, and overspeed safety gear.
4. **Local AMC Service Network:** Partner with manufacturers maintaining direct local engineers in Hyderabad rather than third-party subcontractors.
